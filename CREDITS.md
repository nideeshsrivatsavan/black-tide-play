## Humpback whale: `public/models/whale/`

This is an original procedural model, generated by our own scripts. Its proportions follow
published humpback anatomy, and its side profile was traced from a NOAA Fisheries illustration, a
US government work in the public domain. The reference photographs used while modelling are not included.

## Heads: `public/models/gnm/`

`gnm_head.bin` is derived from Google's **GNM Head** (Generative aNthropometric Model, v3.0),
© 2026 Google LLC, released under the [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0) (a copy is in `public/models/gnm/LICENSE-GNM.txt`):
code at [github.com/google/GNM](https://github.com/google/GNM), weights at
[huggingface.co/google/gnm-v3](https://huggingface.co/google/gnm-v3) (`v3_0/gnm_head.npz`, sha256
`61d78bbf…3747eb`). Changes made by `tools/characters/gnm/bake.py`: the bust cut at the neck and the corneas
removed; the mesh decimated to two levels of detail; the identity basis truncated to 58 components and
quantised; a named expression set and the identity decoder (from GNM's semantic sampler, its output layer
cut to those components, stored as float16) re-encoded; landmarks, shading masks and ambient occlusion
added. No GNM code is included; the runtime (`src/pirate/characters/GnmHead.js`) is our own. Technical
report: S. Ploumpis et al., "GNM Head: A Generative aNthropometric Model of the human head",
arXiv:2607.23687, 2026.

## Bodies: `public/models/body/`

`body.bin` is derived from the **MakeHuman** HM08 base mesh, its macro targets (sex, age, muscle, weight,
three ancestries) and the `game_engine` rig's skin weights, © 2020 Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, Jonas
Hauquier, released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (a copy is in
`public/models/body/LICENSE-MakeHuman-CC0.md`), in the adaptation for MPFB2 shipped with NAVER LABS
Europe's **Anny** body model ([github.com/naver/anny](https://github.com/naver/anny), `src/anny/data/mpfb2`, `3dobjs/base.obj` sha256 `8e761e66…44f6fb4c`,
CC0; Anny's code, Apache 2.0, is not used; its non-commercial SMPL-X data was not downloaded). Paper:
F. Baradel et al., "Human Mesh Modeling for Anny Body", arXiv:2511.03589, 2025. Changes made by
`tools/characters/body/bake.py`: young adult man and woman from the macro targets, fitted to the game's
skeleton and rest pose, the weights moved onto the game's bones, the head removed, the hands and feet
decimated, build deltas (heavier, more muscular, older) added.

## Bodies (MHR): `public/models/body/mhr_body.bin`

`mhr_body.bin` is derived from Meta's **MHR** (Momentum Human Rig), © Meta Platforms, Inc. and affiliates,
released under the [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0) (a copy is in
`public/models/body/LICENSE-MHR.txt`; the same licence is in every release asset zip): code at
[github.com/facebookresearch/MHR](https://github.com/facebookresearch/MHR), release v1.2.0 converted assets
`mhr-assets-v1-common.zip` (sha256 `e8fa874c…71c05724`) and `mhr-assets-v1-lod2.zip` (sha256
`2a555885…1ececc9`). Changes made by `tools/characters/mhr/bake.py`: a man's and a woman's identity, posed
from MHR's A-pose into the game's rest pose with MHR's own skinning and pose correctives, fitted to the game's
skeleton, the weights moved onto the game's bones, the head removed, the hands decimated; twelve identity
components and elbow / knee correctives kept as int16. No MHR code is included; the forward kinematics,
skinning and corrective network are re-implemented in numpy in the bake. Paper: "MHR: Momentum Human Rig",
arXiv:2511.15586, 2025.

## Sea monsters: `public/models/monsters/`

The bodies of the Leviathan, the Kraken, the sea serpent and the Bakunawa, the Isonade and the Lusca are
built from five animal models published on [Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com). Four are photogrammetry scans
of real specimens dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/),
and one is a sculpt under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):

| Model | Author | Licence | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [High-Poly Humpback Whale](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/high-poly-humbpack-whale-5a6a09f1c27e4d0f890f2a30c10d7a81) | K9239 | CC BY 4.0 | the Leviathan's body, flukes and flippers |
| [Black caiman](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/black-caiman-fe345a7b30a7452b976fd1f43262dbea) (*Melanosuchus niger*), a museum specimen | State Darwin Museum, Moscow (darwinmuseum.ru) | CC0 1.0 | the Leviathan's head, jaws and neck |
| [Indonesian Whaler Shark](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cc0-indonesian-whaler-shark-c8c0853a047e4fefa0ce98be353bcf6a) | ffish.asia / floraZia.com (ffishAsia-and-floraZia) | CC0 1.0 | the Isonade's and the Lusca's bodies, fins and jaws |
| [Daian Sea Eel](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cc0-daian-sea-eel-conger-erebennus-a9ebd0c2970449ce80fa3a9b99ba66c3) (*Conger erebennus*) | ffish.asia / floraZia.com (ffishAsia-and-floraZia) | CC0 1.0 | the sea serpent's and the Bakunawa's heads and bodies |
| [North Pacific Giant Octopus](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cc0-north-pacific-giant-octopus-7fb48f6cbcd54149bb23837a3ac09b2e) | ffish.asia / floraZia.com (ffishAsia-and-floraZia) | CC0 1.0 | the Kraken's head and mantle |

The humpback whale is "High-Poly Humpback Whale" by K9239, licensed under CC BY 4.0; it was modified as
described here. The files were taken from the Objaverse 1.0 mirror on Hugging Face (allenai/objaverse), which
redistributes each object under its own licence; the licences were checked on the Sketchfab pages above.
Changes made by `tools/monsters/bake.py` (downloads by `tools/monsters/fetch.py`, kept outside the repo):
the meshes were cut into parts (the whale's flippers, the caiman's head, the octopus's head and mantle
without its arms), straightened, rescaled to the monsters' size, the open jaws closed about a hinge, the
octopus's flattened mantle re-inflated, the caiman's head joined onto the whale's body, decimated, bound to
the game's chain rig, and their colours reduced to a pattern multiplied onto each monster's own palette.
The eyes, teeth, horns, frills, arms and tentacles drawn with them are still procedural. Details are in
[`public/models/monsters/CREDITS.md`](public/models/monsters/CREDITS.md).
